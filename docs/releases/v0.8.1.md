The column schedule at one scale, and a command to draw the schedules again.

## Column schedule: every section at one scale

The column sections are now all drawn at one scale you pick (1 : 20 by default; 1 : 10 to 1 : 50 offered, remembered), written above the schedule. A larger column is drawn larger: at 1 : 20 a 600 mm column is 30 mm wide on paper and a 900 mm one 45 mm. Before, each section was enlarged to fill its cell, so every column looked the same size. The cells grow only when the largest section needs it. The scale is asked in `sdt columns`, `sdt design` and `sdt schedules`.

## New: `sdt schedules`

Draws the beam and girder schedules, the column schedule, or both, again from a stored design (`<model> - design data.pkl` of `sdt beams` / `sdt columns`, or `<model> - DESIGN - design data.pkl` of `sdt design`), with the tie style and the scale chosen now. Nothing is designed again and ETABS is not needed. The design commands still write their schedules as before.

## Fixed

- The v0.7.0 notes and guide said the design data file stays at the top of the output folder; it stays beside the model, as before.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.8.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
