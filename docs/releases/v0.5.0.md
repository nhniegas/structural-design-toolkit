A new command checks the structural irregularities and the P-delta stability coefficient, another gives the concrete quantities and the concrete ratio, and the 3D model can now hold the reinforcing bars. The results of the other commands are the same as in v0.4.0.

## New: irregularity check (`sdt irregularity`)

- Reads the analysed model and reports each irregularity of UBC 97 Tables 16-L and 16-M (NSCP 2015 Tables 208-9 and 208-10) as regular, irregular, or left to you, with the table of numbers behind it.
- **Computed:** soft story, mass, vertical geometric, weak story, torsional irregularity, re-entrant corners and diaphragm openings.
- **Weak story** uses the column design: run `sdt columns` first. Without it the check says so.
- **Ax.** The accidental torsion amplifier of each level is reported with the torsion check. It is not applied to the model.
- **P-delta stability coefficient** of each story, `theta = Px D / (Vx h)`, on first-order drifts: a copy of the model is analysed with P-delta off, and removed. You can also use the drifts of the model as analysed.
- **Left to you**, with what the model shows: in-plane discontinuity, out-of-plane offsets, non-parallel systems.
- The report ends with what follows: whether the static procedure is still allowed, and whether the spectrum scaling is 90 % or 100 %. `sdt analyze` is not changed and still scales to 100 %.
- The model is not changed. The report is saved as `<model> - Irregularity.txt`.

See `docs/etabs_irregularity.md`.

## New: reinforcing bars in the 3D model (`sdt 3d`)

- When a beam or column design is saved for the model, `sdt 3d` asks whether to include the bars: no, the main bars only, or the main bars with the stirrups and ties.
- **Columns:** every longitudinal bar at its designed position, and the ties at the designed spacing with their inner legs. A spiral is drawn as rings.
- **Beams:** the top and bottom bars of each layer, the web bars, and the stirrups at their two spacings.
- The bars are straight and stop at the ends of each member. The designs give no laps, hooks, bends or cut-off points, so none is drawn; extra bars at a support or at midspan are drawn over a fixed share of the clear span.
- Members with no design are drawn without bars and counted.
- On the designed test model: 1,300 solids in 3 MB with the main bars, 6,600 solids in 6.5 MB with stirrups and ties.

See `docs/etabs_3d_model.md`.

## New: concrete quantities (`sdt quantities`)

- The volume of concrete of each story (columns, beams, walls, slabs), the floor area, and the concrete ratio in m3/m2: the total volume over the total floor area.
- Nothing is counted twice: beams are measured below the slab and between the faces of their supports; slabs less their openings and the columns through them.
- **Slab on grade.** It is not modelled as a rule, so its area is the footprint of the building and you type its thickness (0 leaves it out). It counts as the ground floor in the floor area. It is not in the 3D model.
- **Foundation.** The footings and tie beams of the saved foundation plan can be added.
- The model is not changed and no analysis is needed. The report is saved as `<model> - Concrete Quantities.txt` and `.xlsx`.

See `docs/etabs_concrete_quantities.md`.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.5.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
