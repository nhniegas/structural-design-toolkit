## New: girders with a clear span under 4d

A beam of a special moment frame must have a clear span of at least 4 times its effective depth (ACI 18.6.2.1(a)). A shorter girder does not qualify, and the SMRF rules give it a capacity shear that grows as the section grows, so it fails in shear at every size.

With SMRF on, `beams` and `design` now ask what to do with these girders:

- **Apply the SMRF rules to them, as to every girder.** The default, and what earlier versions did.
- **Design them without the SMRF rules.** For the forces of the analysis only: no strength ratios along the span, no 2.5 % steel limit, no probable-moment shear, no hoop spacing of 18.6.4.

Before choosing the second:

- It is your judgement, not a code exemption. Confirm how each girder is meant to resist the earthquake.
- Each of them is also a deep beam by ACI 9.9.1.1 (clear span at most 4h). The toolkit designs it as an ordinary beam; the strut-and-tie or nonlinear strain design and the distributed steel of ACI 9.9 are not covered.
- The columns are not relieved: joint shear, strong column - weak beam and the column capacity shear still count the strength of these girders.

With the option on, the results have a "Seismic rules" column, the summary lists the girders left out, and their calculation sheets say so. `check` now says in its 4d warning that this choice exists, and how many girders it concerns.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.3.2-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
