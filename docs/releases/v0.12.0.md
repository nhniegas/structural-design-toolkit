`sdt model`: the model from a DXF, with the framing of its floors.

## `sdt model` (old name `sdt grids`)

- **The stories, grids, columns and walls** from the DXF, as `sdt grids` built them.
- **Then the framing of every floor,** worked out from the columns and walls:
  - girders between neighbouring columns along X and Y, with a tolerance for columns off a line;
  - girders added so that every column is held along X and Y;
  - a transfer girder under each planted column;
  - trimmer beams around large openings;
  - cantilever beams and an edge beam at every overhang;
  - two-way or one-way slabs bay by bay.
- **Code minimums for deflection without calculation:** NSCP Tables 408.3.1.2, 407.3.1.1 and 409.3.1.1, rounded up to the setup sizes, with b ≥ 0.3 h.
- **Options ranked by concrete volume.** An option comes first when it has no problems and meets the frame action rules:
  - every column held by a girder along X and Y;
  - no one-bay girder line;
  - no girder with a clear span under 4d;
  - the centre of rigidity near the centre of mass.

  The formwork area and the member count are shown beside the volume. Typical floors are framed once.
- **You hold the slab thickness or the beam spacing,** or let the volume decide over every setup thickness. You can give the architect's deepest member for each floor.
- **The drawing gains three layers:**
  - `S-SLAB`: the slab outline, with overhangs;
  - `S-OPENING`: openings;
  - `S-LOAD`: occupancy zones, whose loads are typed once per name.
- **The best options in ETABS:** each is built in a copy of the model and analysed once, giving its drift, periods and maximum-to-average drift. An option over its drift limit by no more than 10 % is marked marginal.
- **The options drawing:** every option drawn side by side in `<model> - framing options.dxf`, with a table in `<model> - framing options.txt`.
- **The option you pick is built:**
  - girders (`G_`) and beams (`B_`);
  - slabs, one-way where the bay is one-way;
  - the loads of each zone on `SIDL` and the live pattern you choose;
  - a rigid diaphragm.

  The sizes are rule sizes for `sdt design` to check and resize.

## Fix
- `sdt drift` (and the drift stage of `sdt design`) on a model with no analysis results read no period of the drift patterns: ETABS makes the patterns that hold it (`EQXSD(1/2)`) at the analysis, after the names were read. The limit then fell back to 0.020 h even for a short period. The names are now read after the analysis.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.12.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
