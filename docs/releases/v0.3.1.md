Fixes and options from the first run on a real project model (2,600 frames, 13 stories).

## Fixed

- **A beam from a column to a girder was taken as a cantilever.** Its clear span came out short and its capacity shear far too large. The support status now looks at what holds each end: a column or wall, a beam across it, or a girder whose centre line passes through the joint, also when ETABS has not split that girder there. A cantilever that ETABS has in two pieces is still a cantilever.
- **The design loop could grow a beam without end.** A beam failing in shear was made larger at every iteration, up to the largest size allowed, because its capacity shear grew with it. A beam made larger 3 times for shear that still fails now keeps its size and is reported.

## New: beam torsion

`beams` and `design` ask which torsion the beams are designed for: the analysis torsion, or at most φTcr (compatibility torsion, ACI 22.7.3.2). The results say which was used. `check` now warns when the beams keep their full torsional stiffness (J modifier near 1), which is what makes the analysis torsion large.

## New: beam bar spacing

`beams` and `design` ask how many bars a beam face needs for spacing: the office rule of at most 150 mm clear, as before, or the code only (crack control, ACI 24.3.2). The extra bars of the office rule raise the probable moments, and with them the capacity shear of the beams and the joint shear and capacity shear of the columns.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.3.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
