The seismic factors that were only reported are now applied: the torsion amplification Ax, the redundancy factor ρ, and the 90 % spectrum scaling for regular structures.

## `sdt analyze`

- **Ax (NSCP 208.5.1.4, Eq. 208-7).** Where a level's largest displacement is over 1.2 times its average, its accidental torsion is multiplied by Ax = (δmax / 1.2 δavg)², between 1.0 and 3.0.
  - The added torsion, (Ax − 1) times the level's accidental torque, is a load pattern of its own beside each static seismic pattern (`AX EQXPE` …). It is a moment on the level's rigid diaphragm, in every combination with its pattern.
  - The spectrum cases take 5 % × the largest Ax as their eccentricity.
  - Ax is read once, from the static cases with their 5 % eccentricity, then the model is analysed with the added torsion. No iteration: the added torsion would raise Ax again each round.
- **ρ (NSCP 208.6.1, Eq. 208-20),** for X and Y: ρ = 2 − 6.1 / (rmax √AB).
  - rmax is the largest element-story shear ratio at or below two-thirds of the height: two adjacent frame columns (70 % for a column with bays on both sides), or a wall's shear × 3 / lw.
  - Limits: between 1.0 and 1.5; 80 % for dual systems; a warning to add bays for special moment frames over 1.25.
  - The ULS and SLS seismic combinations take ρx on the X cases and ρy on the Y cases (`EQ_RHO_nn`, `RSA_RHO_nn`). The Em and drift combinations keep 1.0.
- **90 % scaling (NSCP 208.5.3.5.4).** The spectrum goes to 90 % of the static base shear when the last `sdt irregularity` found the structure regular and you confirm the types it cannot check are absent. Otherwise it stays at 100 %.

## `sdt irregularity`

- Writes `<model> - regularity.json` (Summaries): the irregular types, for `sdt analyze`.

## `sdt setup`

- The `AX` patterns are not asked a role on a model set up again.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.13.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
