## Deflection: clearer results, and the stages of construction

**What the checks are, said plainly.** The check that was labelled "after partitions (long term)" is the deflection after attachment of the partitions as ACI Table 24.2.2 defines it: the long-term deflection of the sustained load plus the live load that is not sustained. It is not the total, and the live load check has no long-term effect in it. The results and the calculation sheets now say so, and show two more values for information:

- the long-term part alone, λΔ Δ(DL + 0.25 LL);
- the total, immediate plus long-term.

**New: when the partitions are built.** `beams`, `deflection` and `design` ask when the partitions are built:

- **Not known** (the default): all the long-term deflection is counted after them, as before. This is the safe side.
- **Enter when they are built**: the months from when the beams first carry their dead load, and the share of the dead load in place by then. The long-term deflection that happens before the partitions is deducted, as the code allows, and the results show the deflection when the dead load is first carried and just before the partitions are built.

The time factor follows ACI Table 24.2.4.1.3 (1.0 at 3 months, 1.2 at 6, 1.4 at 12, 2.0 at 5 years). The staged values use the ACI time-factor method with one cracked stiffness for every stage; the model is not read stage by stage.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.3.3-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
