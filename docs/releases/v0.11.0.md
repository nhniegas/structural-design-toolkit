Walls in the design loop.

## `sdt design`: the walls too

On a model with walls, `sdt design` asks whether to design them in the loop. With Yes, every story of every wall is designed as `sdt walls` designs it, once the beams and columns pass (and the drift, when it is resized for). A wall leg grows 50 mm when:

- its shear is over its limit (NSCP 418.10.4.4, 411.5.4.3);
- the axial load and bending of the wall fail even with the closest bars (every leg of that story);
- it is thinner than its special boundary elements need: hu/16 (418.10.6.4(b)), and 300 mm when c/lw ≥ 3/8 in a wall with hw/lw ≥ 2 (418.10.6.4(c)).

The leg's panels take the wall section of the new thickness (made with the same concrete when the model lacks it). Walls are never made thinner, nor past the largest thickness you give (400 mm offered). After a change every member is sized again. The final wall design is written with the beams and columns: workbook, calculation report and wall schedule.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.11.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
