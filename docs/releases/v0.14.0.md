`sdt model` frames plans with grids at any angle, slab edges and voids of any shape, and a bottom floor of tie beams. Built for the RP Antipolo Exp 2 bottom building, whose wings run at 26 and 36 degrees.

## `sdt model`: the drawing

- **Grid lines drawn as polylines** of two points are read as grid lines (before, only lines were).
- **Arcs** in the slab outline, the openings and the load zones are read as straight pieces of about 2 m (before, an arc was read as its chord).
- **A column drawn twice** (the same shape within 5 mm) is read once, with a warning. **Plain lines on the column layer** are skipped with a warning (before, without one).

## `sdt model`: the framing

- **Girders along the grid lines, at any angle.** Each column frames along the directions of the grid lines through it; bays take the axes of their girders, and their beams run along them. A plan with no grid lines frames along X and Y as before.
- **Slab edges and voids get edge beams** along each straight piece, with cantilever beams out from the columns (up to the length you give, 3 m offered) and, along edges with no support over 12 m, from the columns and girders within 8 m. A slab edge within 1 m of its girder is a plain overhang. Edge stretches nothing can carry are listed as problems.
- **Voids:** a large opening that crosses girders becomes slab edge; the girders stop at it. One inside a bay keeps its trimmer beams.
- **A small opening inside a slab piece** is drawn in ETABS as an opening (before, the slab was drawn over it).
- **A bottom floor with no slab** (slab on grade): tie beams along the girder lines with the `FTB` sizes of the setup, and no slab. Asked when the lowest plan has no slab outline and the others do.
- **No girder longer than the deepest setup girder can span** (ℓ/16), and no frame-action girder shorter than 1.5 m.
- The framing of a floor is worked out once for every thickness and scheme, so ranking the options is faster.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.14.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
