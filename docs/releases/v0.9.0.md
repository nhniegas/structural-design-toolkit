A new command for the members that are not part of the seismic-force-resisting system.

## New: `sdt compatibility`

Checks the members not designated as part of the seismic-force-resisting system, NSCP 2015 208.7.2.3 and 418.14: the members of the ETABS group `GRAVITY ONLY`, and every gravity beam (beams resting on other beams), which are always checked.

- **The design displacement δu:** from an analysis with those members pinned, so that the frames and walls carry the earthquake alone (208.7.2.3), amplified to ΔM = 0.7 R ΔS and at least 0.0025 h, story by story and in each direction. The model is put back afterwards.
- **The forces at δu:** in the model as built, with the members' real end fixity: the forces of the drift load case scaled to δu. These are added to 1.2D + f L (f = 0.5 or 1.0, asked) and to 0.9D (418.14.2.1).
- **The clause:** within φMn and φVn of the member as designed, NSCP 418.14.3.2 (stirrups at d/2 over the whole beam; hoops at so = min(6 db, 150 mm) over the whole column; half the confinement above 0.35 Po); over them, 418.14.3.3 (the special moment frame rules).
- **Without a group** it proposes members for it: girders with a clear span under 4d, cantilevers, planted columns, columns with no girder in a direction, and columns under half the average share of their story's shear. It can create the group with them for you to review in ETABS.
- Writes `Results/<model> - Compatibility.xlsx` and `Summaries/<model> - Compatibility.txt`.

See `docs/etabs_compatibility.md`.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.9.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
