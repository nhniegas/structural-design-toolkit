Speed, smaller report files and the rest of the fixes from the real project run (2,100 beams, 500 columns). The design results do not change: the faster code was compared with the old one on the whole real model, value by value.

## Faster

- **Beam design, about three times.** The forces of each beam are found once, the zones of every combination are cut from arrays, and the envelopes and the beam network are built once. On the real model the beam design with deflection and carrier depth takes about 100 s where it took 295 s. Every value of all 4,228 result rows is the same as before.
- **Fewer analyses in the design loop.** An iteration that follows one with no section change does not analyse or design again: the model is the one the last results were made on. That is the first column iteration after the beams settle and the final check after the columns settle. On the test model a round of six iterations runs four analyses. The log says when it happens.
- **Column design, a first step.** The end forces and the joint forces are read as plain records (about a quarter less time on 489 columns, same results). The interaction surfaces, the slenderness and the joint checks are not yet reworked.

Every iteration still designs every member on the forces of its own analysis, and every round still ends with a full check of all members. The check of a passing member with its existing bars, in place of a new design, is not in this version.

## Smaller files

- **Column calculation report.** The interaction figures are compressed images at 110 dpi: about 24 MB of figures for 497 columns, where it was 107 MB.
- **Column schedule DXF.** The curved corners of hoops and ties are drawn with as few points as keep them within 0.05 mm of the true outline. The file is about a third of its size.

## Drift

- **A model with no DRIFT combinations** can be checked on the drift load cases themselves (`EQXSD`, `EQYSD` and the wind patterns). They carry the forces of the period without its cap, which is what NSCP 208.6.5.2 allows for drift. Combinations of your own usually carry the strength forces and give a larger drift than the code asks for. No gravity load acts with the cases, so the P-delta effect of the combinations is not in the values, and the report says so.
- **The service level stiffness is an input,** asked with the wind limit: the factor on the strength level, 1.4 by ACI 6.6.3.2.2. A member modelled stiffer than the service level keeps its own stiffness, so a beam at 0.5 Ig is not set to 0.49.
- **Typed Ca and Cv: the zone 4 minimum.** With the coefficients typed in, ETABS keeps no zone or Nv and applies 0.8 Z Nv I / R (Eq. 208-11) with Nv = 1. Near a source that is too low, and so is the drift of a pattern the minimum governs. The site the two values belong to is found again from the tables. `analyze`, `drift` and the design loop scale the drift load cases up to the minimum with the real Nv after each analysis (the factor follows the period and goes back to 1 when the minimum does not govern), and the spectrum drift cases are scaled to them as before. `check` fails a pattern that is still under the minimum. A model whose patterns are defined by their site (a blank model set up with `sdt setup`) needs none of this: ETABS has the Nv.

## Design loop

- **Members that no size fixes** are listed apart in the log and the summary, by what stops them: at the largest size allowed, shear that grows with the section, or a deflection that the support governs.
- **Where the time goes.** Each iteration in the log ends with its time and how it divides: analysis, reading the results, beam design, column design, choosing the sizes and resizing.
- **Drift is not sized for.** The loop says so when it starts, and the drift check at the end reports it.
- **When the loop is done.** The guide now says what "converged" means (a full check of every member that changes nothing) and what "optimal" means here: no trimmed member can lose another size without failing. It is not a least-weight design.

## Other

- The model is named by its `.EDB` in the messages and file names after an analysis, where ETABS gives the `.$et` text copy.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.3.5-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
