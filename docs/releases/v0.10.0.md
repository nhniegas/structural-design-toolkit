Structural wall design.

## New: `sdt walls`

Designs the structural walls of the model open in ETABS, planar, L, T, U and cores, to NSCP 2015 418.10 (special structural walls) or 411 (walls), chosen wall by wall.

- **Legs and walls:** every straight leg has its own pier label (`W1A`, `W1B`, ...), kept from story to story; the legs that meet are one wall, designed as one section with its flanges (418.10.5.1).
- **Forces:** the pier forces of the legs summed by statics to the centroid of the wall; the convention was checked in ETABS against a pier of the whole wall (agreement to 2e-5).
- **Axial load and bending:** strain compatibility on the assembled section about each principal axis; the vertical bars come closer until every strength combination passes.
- **Shear, leg by leg:** Eq. 418.10.4.1 with αc, the limits of 418.10.4.4, and φ 0.60 or 0.75 by 421.2.4.1; ordinary walls by 411.5.4.
- **Minimum steel and curtains:** 418.10.2.1 and 418.10.2.2; Table 411.6.1 and 411.6.2.
- **Special boundary elements:** the displacement method (418.10.6.2, with δu from the drift load cases) and the stress method (418.10.6.3), both given; you choose which decides. Lengths rounded up to your module.
- Writes `Results/<model> - Wall Design.xlsx`.

`sdt tag` now also gives the walls their pier labels.

Not yet: resizing the wall thickness in `sdt design`, coupling beams, the DXF wall schedule and the PDF report. See `docs/etabs_wall_design.md`.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.10.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
