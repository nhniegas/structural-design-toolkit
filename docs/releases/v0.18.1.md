The progress window builds a city, the command window stays where you are, and the calculation reports take the look of ETABS.

## The progress window

- The moving bar is replaced by a city built along the loading line: a tower crane runs back and forth along it, and the buildings its hook passes go up floor by floor, drawn as structures in the blue of ETABS (the floors that are done filled in light blue). On each pass some buildings take another height, and now and then one is taken down and another built in its place, so the city keeps changing while the command works. The step being done is still written under it.
- **Seven kinds of building:** moment frames, braced frames (an X in each bay), setback towers, high-rises with a spire, houses with a pitched roof, warehouses with a roof truss, and domed buildings.
- **Ten landmarks**, as simple outlines among them: the Burj Khalifa, Taipei 101 (with its tuned mass damper ball swinging in the top), the Petronas Towers and their skybridge, the Eiffel Tower, the Sydney Opera House, the Empire State Building, the Leaning Tower of Pisa, the CN Tower, the Gateway Arch and the Colosseum.
- A frame that cannot be drawn is skipped and the animation goes on: it never stops while the command works (`tests/test_loading_city.py` runs it for thousands of frames).

## The command window

- **The page no longer jumps to the bottom.** With a question still waiting further down (an output folder never chosen), each change of an answer scrolled the page down to it. The page now stays where it was; the form scrolls to a waiting question only the first time it shows it.
- **Smoother scrolling.** The mouse wheel moves the page in steps of a few lines, and turns that come in while the page is redrawn are scrolled at once instead of one after the other.

## Calculation reports

- The reports take the look of the windows and the icon: near-black text, the blue of ETABS for the rule under the header and the section marks, and the `SDT` mark white on blue. PASS and FAIL keep their green and red.
- **The summary table fits the page.** The beam summary was 175 mm wide on a 174 mm line and ran past the right margin; the column summary was 179 mm. Both are narrower now, and a test keeps every summary table within the margins.

## Girder bars through the columns

- Stated and tested: the girders whose support bars are matched to the next span (v0.17.0) are checked again with those bars in full, the 2.5 % steel limit (NSCP 418.6.3.1; ACI 18.6.3.1), the probable-moment shear Ve, the stirrups and the deflection, and `sdt design` does it at every iteration, so a girder that fails with its matched bars is resized. Nothing changed in the design; the guide now says so and two tests hold it.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.18.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
