Fixes from the backlog: files sorted into folders, NSCP 2015 cited first, safer defaults, the seismic system by name, and `sdt setup` and `sdt grids` made safe on models built elsewhere.

## Output folders

Every command now sorts what it writes into subfolders: `Results` (workbooks), `Calculations` (PDF reports), `Schedules` (beam and column schedules), `Drawings` (framing plans and the 3D model) and `Summaries` (summaries, logs, drift and irregularity reports). The commands that write beside the model (drift, irregularity, quantities, foundation reactions, the plan changes of `sdt grids`, the log of `sdt design`) use the same subfolders in the model's folder. The design data file stays beside the model, where the later commands read it. See `docs/output_folders.md`.

## NSCP 2015 cited first

The concrete design commands, their workbooks, PDF reports, summaries and guides now cite NSCP 2015 with ACI beside it: "NSCP 418.6.2.1 (ACI 18.6.2.1)", or "(NSCP 424.2; ACI 24.2)" inside parentheses. Every one of the 115 clauses the program cites was checked in the NSCP 2015 text: NSCP chapter 4 numbers each clause as 4 and the ACI 318-14 number. The design code shown in the reports is "NSCP 2015 chapter 4 (ACI 318M-14)". The steel, composite and wind commands are unchanged.

## Changed defaults

- **Beam bar spacing:** the code spacing (crack control, NSCP 424.3.2) is now the default; the office rule of 150 mm clear between bars stays as an option. A user who chose the office rule last time is offered it first.
- **Drift:** `sdt drift` reads the drift at the outer corners by default. The corners give the largest drift with its torsion and read the stories that have no diaphragm; the centre of mass stays as an option.

## The wind both ways

The strength and service combinations of `sdt setup` now have every wind case with `+ W` and `- W` (`ULS 108 0.9 DL - 1.0 WX`, ...), and the wind drift combinations a reversed one too: the wind blows either way, and 0.9 D − W governs the uplift. A model set up again gets 20 more combinations.

## The seismic system, from NSCP Table 208-11A

R alone does not name a system: 5.5 is the intermediate moment frame (not permitted in zone 4), the dual system with ordinary walls (not permitted) and the shear wall-frame interaction system (permitted to 50 m). The earlier table keyed by R had these wrong, and used Ω0 = 2.8 for every system.

- `sdt setup` asks for the system from the list of Table 208-11A; R and Ω0 come from it. Ω0 is used in the special seismic combinations (2.2 for ordinary walls of a building frame, 2.5 for intermediate precast walls, 2.0 for cantilevered columns).
- `sdt check` checks the system against the zone and the building height against its limit (10, 50 or 75 m). A model with no walls can only be a frame system. When R can still be more than one system, the check asks which and saves the answer with the model.
- Setup inputs saved before v0.7.0 have only R: the first system with that R is taken.

## `sdt setup` on a model with load patterns of its own

The patterns of the model that are not the setup's own (`Dead`, `SDL`, `Live`, ...) are listed, each with what it is: super dead, live, reducible live, roof live, or left out. They then go into the combinations, the seismic mass and the P-delta load. Before, they were left out of all of them, and a `Dead` pattern with self weight 1.0 counted the self weight twice; it now gets 0, with a note.

## `sdt grids` on a model with members

- When the drawing names its stories differently from the model (`2F` against `L2`), the stories are paired first (in order, or by level within 300 mm) and the pairing is shown in the confirmation. Before, every column of the model was removed. When they cannot be paired, nothing is changed.
- The lines of the model's other grid systems (a rotated wing, an offset grid) are kept. Before, they were rewritten into the first system.

## A model keeps its units

The commands work in N-mm whatever the units of the model. A model a command saved (the copies of `sdt design`, `sdt tag`, `sdt foundation`, `sdt grids`, `sdt setup`, and the models `sdt analyze` and `sdt drift` save) then opened in N-mm, because ETABS stores the present units in the file. Every save now puts the model's own units back first.

## Column capacity shear shown in full

The column workbook and calculation report show the Mpr at the bottom, the Mpr at the top and the clear height lu that Ve = (Mpr,bot + Mpr,top) / lu was taken from, so Ve can be checked by hand. The capacity shear of the ground floor corner column, about 2.6 times too large in the v0.1.1 test, does not occur on the test model: there the corner columns have the lowest Ve of their story, as they should, since the beams framing in limit it.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.7.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
