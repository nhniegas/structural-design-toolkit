Slab design: `sdt slabs` designs the solid slabs of the model panel by panel, one-way and two-way, to NSCP 2015, with a schedule, a calculation report and slab marks on the framing plans.

## `sdt slabs`

- **Panels from the model.** The floor objects with a slab property are cut along the beam and wall lines into panels; one slab object over many bays is cut into its bays. Each panel has its own axes, so the bays of a skewed grid are rectangles in theirs. The thickness and f'c come from the slab property; the dead and live loads from the load sets and uniform loads on the floor objects, by load pattern type, so any pattern names work. Nothing in the model is changed, and no analysis is needed.
- **One-way slabs**, supported on two opposite sides or longer than twice their width:
  - the moments and shears of NSCP Table 406.5.2 and Table 406.5.4 when the strip meets 406.5.1;
  - otherwise, a strip analysis with the live load arranged by 406.4.2.
- **Two-way slabs** use ACI Method 3, cases 1 to 9 as in the office slab sheets (NSCP 408.2.1).
  - On beams with a mean alpha_f under a limit you set (2.0 by default), the Direct Design Method of NSCP 408.10 is used when its limits hold.
  - When the DDM limits do not hold, Method 3 is kept and the panel is flagged for a check in ETABS.
- **Cantilevers** are designed as one-way cantilevers.
- **Checks.** Each panel is checked for:
  - thickness: NSCP Tables 407.3.1.1, 408.3.1.2 and 408.3.1.1;
  - flexure and the net tensile strain (0.004 for two-way slabs);
  - minimum steel and the spacing limits;
  - shear without stirrups.

  Failing panels are flagged, not resized.
- **Marks.** Panels of the same design share a mark (`S-1`, numbered on each floor; the schedule groups the marks by floor). Marks whose spacings are all within 25 mm (a window input) are merged, then the closest marks while a floor has more than 15 (also an input), keeping the closer spacing. A failing panel never shares a mark with a passing one.
- **Outputs:**
  - the results workbook: a schedule per floor, every panel and the flags, with failing panels red;
  - the slab schedule DXF: TOP DISCO and CONT, BOTTOM MIDSPAN, short and long, remarks;
  - the calculation report PDF;
  - the panels and marks saved for `sdt plans`.
- **Method 3 tables.** They are taken from SLAB DESIGNER v1.0.xls and checked against SLAB DESIGN.xls.
  - **SLAB DESIGN.xls:** four cells break the smooth run of their columns:
    - Ca,neg case 9 m 0.80: 0.072 (should be 0.075);
    - Ca,dl case 2 m 0.90: 0.0252 (0.022);
    - Ca,dl case 6 m 0.85: 0.0472 (0.042);
    - Cb,ll case 9 m 0.80: 0.0174 (0.017).
  - **SLAB DESIGNER v1.0.xls:** the load ratio table has its short and long direction labels swapped.

  The program uses the corrected values. The sheets themselves were not changed.

## `sdt plans`

- **New:** after `sdt slabs`, the framing plans can show the slab marks (`S-1`; the kind is in the slab schedule) and the office span symbol, on the layer `S-SLAB`. The symbol is a line with a half arrowhead at each end, on opposite sides: along the main bars of a one-way slab, crossed on a two-way slab, with the arrowhead at the free end only on a cantilever. Mark and symbol are centred in the panel along its axes, and the text is made smaller on a narrow bay. Asked only when the slab design was saved.

## Tested

- `tests/test_slabs.py` (28 tests):
  - the Method 3 tables;
  - Table 406.5.2;
  - the strip analysis against w l²/8 and 0.0957 w l²;
  - the strip steel by hand;
  - the thickness tables;
  - the DDM shares;
  - the cut of one slab object into bays;
  - a floor designed by the DDM;
  - the marks, the 25 mm merge and the failing panels kept apart;
  - the slab marks on the plans.
- In ETABS:
  - `t34 slabs.json` runs `sdt slabs` and `sdt plans` (53 panels) against a saved reference.
  - `bottom slabs.json` runs on the BOTTOM model: 259 floor objects into 1,233 panels on a grid skewed about 55 degrees, 860 one-way and 369 two-way, in about a minute. 401 panels are flagged, most of them the 125 mm slab under the NSCP minimum thickness for its span.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.19.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
