Three fixes and one new choice, found designing a one-story mezzanine.

## `sdt analyze`

- **rho on a one-story building.** rmax is taken over the stories at or below two-thirds of the height. A story was counted only when its level (its top) was below two-thirds of the height, so a one-story building had no story at all and rho came out 1.0 with rmax 0. A story now counts when it starts below two-thirds of the height: a one-story building counts its story, and a taller one also the story that crosses two-thirds of the height.

## `sdt design`, `sdt beams`

- A model with no frame releases stopped the force extraction: ETABS has no release table then. Such a model now simply has no releases.
- A model with no circular frame section stopped the extraction the same way (no circle section table); it now has none.

## `sdt columns`, `sdt design`: BCC and joint shear only at the topmost level

The question "Run the strong column - weak beam (BCC), joint shear and probable-moment shear (Ve) checks at the topmost level?" has a third answer: **BCC and joint shear only - leave those out; keep Ve**. The joints on top of the topmost columns are then not checked for BCC and joint shear, but those columns are still designed for the probable-moment shear Ve (NSCP 418.7.6). For a one-story frame, whose columns are both the topmost and the bottom-most. The summary says what was left out.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.13.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
