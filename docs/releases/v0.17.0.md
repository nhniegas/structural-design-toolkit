Girder bars through the columns: `sdt beams` and `sdt design` can match the support bars of girders in line at a column, so they run through it instead of anchoring in it.

## Girder bars through the columns

A new question in `sdt beams` and `sdt design`, off by default (earlier designs do not change):

- **Which girders.** Two girders that meet at a column with their axes within 1 in 6 of a straight line, in plan. Gravity beams keep their own bars.
- **Layer by layer.** At that column, each layer of the top and bottom bars takes the larger count of the two spans, so every bar has its partner in the same place: 4 + 2 and 5 + 0 both become 5 + 2.
- **A narrower girder** takes the most bars that fit in its width; the extra bars of the wider girder anchor in the column, as before.
- **Bottom bars at a depth step** are matched only when the step bends at 1 in 6 or flatter over the smaller side of the column (up to 100 mm at a 600 mm column). Otherwise only the top bars are matched.
- **Designed again.** The matched girders are designed again with these bars: more bars raise the probable-moment shear Ve and the joint demand on the columns, and the SMRF strength ratios may add bottom bars. The matching repeats until no count changes. In `sdt design` this happens at every iteration of the loop.
- **Where it shows.** A results column "Support bars through the columns" (for example `right (to 2GX-2): top 5+2 through; bottom 3 through`), the calculation report, `THRU L` / `THRU R` in the Remarks of the girder schedule with a note under the table, and a line in the closing summary.

The bar layout of a beam end can now have fewer bars in its outer layer than fit (4 + 2 where 6 fit in one layer), and the capacity is computed with the bars in the layers where they are.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.17.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
