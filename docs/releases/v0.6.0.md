A new standalone command: `sdt pilecap`, the design of a pile cap under one column to NSCP 2015.

## New: `sdt pilecap`

- **One window** with every input in a scrolling form, a plan of the cap and its piles that redraws as you type, and the results beside them. The window stays open: change a value and run again. After a run the plan shows the reaction of each pile, any uplift, and the critical sections.
- **Layouts**: standard groups of 2 to 12 piles, or any group by its coordinates. **Loads** typed, or read from an Excel or CSV file with every combination, service and factored.
- **Pile reactions** of a rigid cap with pinned piles, from P, Mx, My, Vx and Vy, the cap's own weight, the soil over it and a surcharge.
- **Pile capacity** by one of three methods: allowable; allowable with the one-third increase for wind and earthquake (NSCP 203.4.2); factored reactions against a factored resistance.
- **Pile heads** pinned or fixed. Fixed heads take M = H Lf / 2 with the depth to fixity of NSCP 306.6; the anchorage of NSCP 308.4.1 is reported.
- **Bars** designed for flexure at the column face, with the minimum steel, uniform short bars, the maximum spacing, and straight or hooked ends by their development length. **Top bars** wherever a section has tension on top (NSCP 414.1.5).
- **Shear**: one-way and two-way at the column, two-way around piles, groups of piles and corner piles, one-way across a corner pile.
- **Deep caps** (piles close to the column): a strut-and-tie model (NSCP 423), or the special checks of the CRSI Design Guide for Pile Caps, as you choose.
- **Column bearing** on the cap (NSCP 422.8.3, φ = 0.65, √(A2/A1) ≤ 2), with the area of column bars or dowels needed for any excess (NSCP 416.3.1.2, at least 0.005 Ag).
- **The least thickness** that passes, on a 50 mm step.
- **Recommendations** of the CRSI guide and of AASHTO LRFD (as quoted by FHWA GEC 12) for pile spacing, edge distance and embedment, shown without failing the design.
- **A PDF report** with a plan of the cap, the reactions of every pile in every combination, and each check with its equation, numbers, clause and result.

NSCP 2015 governs. The size effect factor of ACI 318-19 is not used: NSCP 2015 has none.

**Checked against the CRSI Design Guide for Pile Caps.** Design Examples #1 (16 piles) and #2 (5 piles) were worked again with the program. The reactions, moments and shears match CRSI's exactly. The capacities match once CRSI's φ = 0.85 is changed to NSCP's 0.75. Where the two part ways, NSCP is followed: piles near a section counted in proportion (413.4.2.5), and the perimeter around piles stopped at the cap edge. Both examples are worked step by step in the guide and kept as tests.

See `docs/pile_cap_design.md`.

## Changed: `sdt composite` and `sdt steel` open the same window

- **`sdt composite`**: the tube drawn to scale with its concrete fill; after a run, the interaction diagrams of DG6 2.5.6 about each axis with your demand as a dot, and the ratios of the checks as bars.
- **`sdt steel`**: the section drawn to scale from the AISC table, redrawn when you type another name; after a run, the ratio of every check as a bar, with the limit state that governs each.
- In both, the inputs are in one form (drop-down lists for the choices), the results stay on screen, and you change a value and run again. Print in the terminal and Save PDF report are buttons. The calculations are unchanged.
- The form shows only the inputs that apply to the choices made (in `sdt pilecap`: the pile coordinates only for the coordinates layout, the depth to fixity only for fixed heads, and so on), and a box that may be left blank says what a blank means.
- With an answers file the dialogs are used as before.

## Changed

- The standalone commands can ask their inputs over several dialogs, one after the other, and choose a file after them (`utilities/design_cli.py`); `sdt pilecap` uses them when run from an answers file. `sdt composite`, `sdt steel` and `sdt wind` are unchanged.
- `sdt doctor` checks that the code of `sdt pilecap` loads.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.6.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
