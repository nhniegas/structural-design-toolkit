Two fixes: the P-M diagrams of the columns in each plane, and stories with no diaphragm in the irregularity check.

## `sdt columns`, `sdt design` and the project report

- **P-M diagrams in the XZ and YZ planes.** After its tables, each column of the calculation report now has the two sections of its design interaction surface: φPn against φMnx (My = 0) and φPn against φMny (Mx = 0), with the Pu and the Mx or My of every combination at both ends. The 3D surface stays beside the transverse reinforcement table.

## `sdt irregularity`

- **Stories with no diaphragm are left out and listed.** A story where no floor and no joint has a diaphragm assigned by name (a tie beam level, a level with no slab) is no longer compared for stiffness, mass, geometry, strength or torsion: its mass, stiffness and drift are not those of a floor. The report lists it under "Levels not compared as stories". A model with no diaphragm at all is compared as before.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.11.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
