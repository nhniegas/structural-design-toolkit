Drawings to the office standard: the framing plans and every schedule DXF follow the ECC drafting standard, so they go onto an ECC sheet and plot with `ECC_FULL for Paper.ctb` with no re-layering.

## The ECC drafting standard in every DXF

- **Colour = pen.** Every object is ByLayer on an ECC `S-` layer. The layer colour is its pen in `ECC_FULL for Paper.ctb`:
  - columns `S-COLS` 5 (0.53 mm);
  - girders `S-GIRDER` 4 (0.40, hidden) and beams `S-BEAM` 3 (0.15, hidden);
  - walls `S-WALL` 4; footings `S-FOOTING` 6 (hidden); tie beams `S-TIEBEAM` 4 (hidden);
  - text `S-ANNO-TEXT`, grid bubbles `S-ANNO-SYMB`, slab marks `S-ANNO-SLAB`: 52 (0.13);
  - dimensions `S-ANNO-DIMS` 1 (0.18); grid lines `S-GRID` 251 (0.09 grey, center);
  - schedules: concrete `S-DETAIL` 2, bars `S-REBAR` 5, ties `S-DETL-RBAR` 5, outlines and headers `S-SCHEDULE` 2, table lines `S-ANNO-SYMB`, titles `S-REFR-TITL-1`.
- **No lineweights** on objects or layers: the plot style gives them. Opened without the CTB, the drawings show the layer colours.
- **Text** in the SIMPLEX style (simplex.shx) at width 0.85; condensed text in the tables keeps its width.
- The layer names before v0.20.0 (`S-COLUMN`, `S-TEXT`, `S-DIM`, `S-SLAB`, `TABLE`, `CONCRETE`, ...) are gone from the outputs. Girders and beams swapped colours: the girders are now the heavier pen.
- One module holds the standard: `utilities/ecc_drafting.py`. The standard itself, with its template, plot styles and tools, is in `Desktop\ECC DRAFTING STANDARDS`.

## `sdt plans`

- **Grid labels** are black text (`S-ANNO-TEXT`), their bubbles on `S-ANNO-SYMB`; before, both were grey with the grid lines.
- **Slab marks in narrow bays.** A one-way slab's mark is centred above its span symbol, and the two sit a quarter of the bay's length off its middle, clear of the beam names written at the middle of the beams.
- The dimension style draws its lines, arrows and text by layer, in SIMPLEX.

## `sdt model` / `sdt grids`

- Columns drawn on `S-COLS` (the ECC column layer) are read too, while the column layer keeps its default `S-COLUMN`. A layer named in the window replaces both.
