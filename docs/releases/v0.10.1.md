Coupling beams, the wall schedule and the wall calculation report.

## `sdt walls`

- **Coupling beams:** a beam joining two separate walls is designed by NSCP 418.10.7: as a special moment frame beam when ln/h ≥ 4, with two groups of diagonal bars when ln/h < 2 and Vu > 0.33 λ √f'c Acw (Avd = Vu / (0.85 · 2 fy sin α)), either otherwise; Vn at most 0.83 √f'c Acw.
- **Degree of coupling** of every pair of walls joined by coupling beams, under each drift load case: T L / (T L + M1 + M2). Under the limit you give (20 % offered) the walls act as separate cantilevers.
- **Wall schedule (DXF):** one section detail per wall and story, a wall whose legs meet (L, U, core) drawn as one section, at the scale you give, with the vertical bars, the special boundary element zones and rows for the bars.
- **Calculation report (PDF),** like the column report: shear by leg, axial load and bending about each principal axis with the P-M curves and the demands, minimum steel, both boundary element checks.
- Boundary elements are no longer checked for bending out of the plane of every leg.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.10.1-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
