## New: framing plans as DXF

`plans` writes the framing plans of the model open in ETABS as one DXF, the floors side by side at 1 : 1 in mm.

- **Beams and girders** are dashed multilines at their true width. A multiline keeps its width when an end point is dragged in AutoCAD. Each one stops at the face of the column, or of the girder that carries it.
- **Columns** are solid at their true size and rotation; **walls** at their thickness.
- **Marks:** every beam has its name; a column has its mark without the level (`C1`), on every floor or only where it starts.
- **Grids**, optional, with bubbles and, optionally, the dimensions between them.
- Layers: `S-GIRDER`, `S-BEAM`, `S-COLUMN`, `S-WALL`, `S-GRID`, `S-TEXT`, `S-DIM`.

The model is not changed. Slabs are not drawn yet. See `docs/etabs_framing_plans.md` in the zip.

This version includes every fix of 0.1.2.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.2.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
