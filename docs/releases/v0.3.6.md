Column sizing in the design loop. On a real model the loop of v0.3.5 sent 42 columns to the largest size allowed (2000 mm) in one iteration, and about 160 more followed because a column is never smaller than the one above it. They were far larger than needed: 1 % steel and a median flexure ratio of 0.34.

## Fixed: columns sent to the largest size

When a column fails a member check, the loop tries the next sizes on the forces of the analysis just run and takes the first that passes. Two things were wrong:

- **The trial was stricter than the design.** Its capacity shear Ve came from the column's own probable moments over its whole length. The design takes the clear height between the beams and stops Ve at what the beams at the joints can deliver (ACI 18.7.6.1.1). Without that, Ve grows faster than the shear strength of the section, and no size passes. The trial now uses the clear height and the beam limits of the last design.
- **When no size passed, the column went to the largest size.** It now grows one size, and the log names the check that stopped the largest size tried.

## New: a guard for column shear that a larger section does not help

A column that fails in shear alone, was made larger for it, and whose shear ratio came down by less than 5 % keeps its size and is reported with both ratios, under "Not fixed by a larger size (shear: the capacity shear grows with the section)". A column below it that only followed it keeps its own size. Beams already had this guard.

## Changed: the topmost level choice also covers Ve

The question "Run the capacity-design checks at the topmost level?" covered strong column-weak beam (BCC) and joint shear. Answering No now also designs the topmost columns for the analysis shear, not the probable-moment shear Ve, as the foundation level choice already did. The results say `Not used - topmost level (user choice): analysis shear`. It is asked in `sdt columns` and `sdt design`.

If you had answered No before, the topmost columns of an SMRF model now get less shear reinforcement than in v0.3.5. Answer Yes to keep Ve.

## What to do with a model sized by v0.3.5

Do not continue from its `- DESIGN.EDB`: the oversized columns changed the stiffness and the forces around them. Start the loop again from the original model.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.3.6-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
