The design loop can now size the members for drift.

## `sdt design`: resize members for drift

A new question before the loop: **Resize members for drift?** With Yes (the default, remembered for next time), once every member passes for strength and deflection, the loop runs `sdt drift` on the current sizes. For each story over a drift limit, in the direction of its load case:

- the columns of that story grow one size, on the side along the drift direction (a circular column grows its diameter);
- the girders along that direction at the floor above the story and at the floor below it grow one depth.

Every member is then sized again for strength, and the drift checked again, until every story passes. The seismic drift (ΔM = 0.7 R ΔS against 0.025 or 0.020 h, NSCP 208.6.5.1) and the wind drift (against your limit) both drive it, at every stiffness level `sdt drift` checks. Members that grew are not made smaller again.

The loop stops when nothing on a failing story can grow (the members are at the largest sizes of the loop limits), or after as many drift resizings as the rounds limit; the summary then says so and names the members at their largest size. The log lists each failing story with its share of the limit, and each change with its reason (`grow for drift (2F X, 1.30 of the limit)`).

With No, the loop is as before: drift is checked on the final sizes and reported.

See `docs/etabs_design_loop.md`.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.8.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
