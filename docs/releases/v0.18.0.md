One window per command, in the look of ETABS: every question of a command is in one window that fills itself in, follows your answers and waits for Run.

## One window for the questions

Before, a command asked its questions one window after the other, and a wrong turn meant cancelling and starting again. Now:

- **All the questions in one window**, one under the other in a page that scrolls, grouped under headings. Each one is already answered with what you last chose, the program's default, or the folder you used before, so the window opens filled in and you change only what you want.
- **What still needs you is outlined**: a list with nothing selected, a folder never chosen, a box with no value. The bottom line says which one.
- **Answers that depend on others follow them.** Change an answer and the questions after it are asked again with it: a question that no longer applies goes away (SMRF set to No: no question about girders under 4d), one that now applies appears, and a list offers what the new answer allows. Your other answers are kept.
- **A value the command refuses** is said in red in the window, which stays open until you change it.
- **Run** turns blue when everything is answered. The model is not changed, and no result is written, before Run; **Cancel** stops with nothing changed.
- A question that can only come once the work has started (the framing of `sdt model` after the grids are built, the members `sdt compatibility` proposes after its analysis) is still asked in a window of its own.
- `SDT_DIALOGS=1` keeps the dialogs one after the other. With `--answers` there is no window, as before; `--record` saves the answers of the window when you press Run.
- `sdt analyze` saves the copy of the model, and `sdt walls` labels the piers, after Run instead of between the questions.

## The look of ETABS

- Every window (the command window, progress, summary, warnings, and the windows of `sdt composite`, `sdt steel` and `sdt pilecap`) now has the light look of ETABS: white, light grey bands and thin grey borders, black Segoe UI text, and the blue of ETABS for the mark, the choice made and the button that confirms.
- The program's icon is redrawn in the same colours: white letters and drawing on the blue of ETABS.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.18.0-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
