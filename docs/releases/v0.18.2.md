Girders resized for the bars matched through the columns, said so in the log; two guides brought up to date.

## Girder bars through the columns in `sdt design`

- A girder whose support bars are matched to the next span (v0.17.0) is checked with those bars at every iteration. When they take it over the 2.5 % steel limit (NSCP 418.6.3.1; ACI 18.6.3.1) or over three layers, the loop makes it deeper; when their larger probable moments raise the capacity shear Ve past what the stirrups can carry, it makes it wider. The columns at its ends are checked for joint shear and strong column - weak beam with the matched bars.
- **New:** the loop log says when the matched bars are part of the reason, for example `grow (smrf steel ratio, with the bars matched through the columns)`. The stop for beams that keep failing in shear, and for deflection, treats these reasons as before.
- Tested in ETABS with a new scenario, `t34 design continuity`: `sdt design` on t34 with the option on (38 column joints, 65 girders matched). No t34 girder reaches 2.5 % with its matched bars, so the final sizes are those of the run with the option off; the resizing itself is held by a test that designs a girder with the bars of a heavy next span and has the loop make it deeper.
- On the larger BOTTOM model (795 girders matched at 563 joints), seven beam iterations of `sdt design` resized girders for their matched bars: those over 2.5 % went from 30 in the first iteration to 2 in the seventh, and those failing shear from 26 to 2. Nineteen girders whose Ve kept rising with their size, since a larger section takes more matched bars, were stopped after three enlargements for shear and reported, rather than grown on.
- The note is only given for the checks the matched bars affect (steel ratio, layers, SMRF moment ratios, shear), not for deflection, which more bars reduce.

## Documentation

- `sdt setup` guide: the spectrum is scaled to the static base shear by `sdt analyze` (the guide still said it was not done).
- `sdt check` guide: the redundancy factor ρ is worked out and applied by `sdt analyze` (it was still listed as not checked). Minimum live loads, partitions and the wind speed against the site map remain unchecked.

## Download and run

1. Download `sdt-0.18.2-windows.zip` below and unzip it. Keep the files of the `sdt` folder together.
2. Double-click `sdt.exe`, then run `doctor` first.

No Python is needed. ETABS is needed for the ETABS commands, and a LaTeX distribution such as MiKTeX for the PDF reports.
