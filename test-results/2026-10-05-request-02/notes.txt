NOTES ON REQUEST 02
===================

How this was run
----------------
Same PC and method as 2026-10-05/: ETABS Ultimate 22.6.0, toolkit 0.1.1 (main
checkout), the real commands in a private ETABS, dialogs answered by a script.
Each dialog is printed with its options, its default and the answer given:
"[default]" = default accepted, "[chosen for this test]" = the answer this
request asked for. Run on 2026-10-05, 00:15 to 00:21. Every item used a fresh
copy of the same 3-storey test model, opened with N-mm present units.


A. Drift: which story height
----------------------------
GF has no diaphragm (A1), so your reading of center_drifts is right: 2F is measured
from the base, over 5.5 m. Numbers for the strength level (0.35 / 0.70), N-mm:

                              EQXSD               EQYSD               wind (WX = WY)
  sdt drift, centre of mass   0.00265 at 2F       0.00255 at 2F       0.00124 at 2F
  sdt drift, outer corners    0.00278 at 2F       0.00301 at 2F       0.00130 at GF
  ETABS Story Drifts (max)    0.002785 at 2F      0.003012 at 2F      0.001304 at GF
  ETABS Story Drifts at GF    0.002761            0.002938            0.001304

  Governing combinations: DRIFT 101 (1.2 + Ev) DL + f LL - 1.0 EQXSD,
  DRIFT 100 (1.2 + Ev) DL + f LL + 1.0 EQYSD, WDRIFT 101 1.2 DL + f LL + 0.5 Lr + 1.0 WX.

Centre of mass displacement of 2F in those combinations (A2, last table):
  UX = 14.559 mm, UY = 13.999 mm.
  14.559 / 5500 = 0.002647 and 13.999 / 5500 = 0.002545: exactly what sdt prints, so
  it does divide the 2F displacement by 5.5 m.
  Over 4.5 m with nothing subtracted it would be 0.003235 and 0.003111. Those are the
  values the kN-m run printed (0.00323, 0.00311), so in kN-m the height came out as 4.5.

My arithmetic, not a toolkit result: neither 5.5 m nor 4.5 m with a zero start is the
2F story drift, because GF moves. Taking the GF displacement as its ETABS story drift
times 1.0 m (2.76 mm in X, 2.94 mm in Y):
  X: (14.559 - 2.76) / 4500 = 0.00262      Y: (13.999 - 2.94) / 4500 = 0.00246
So on this model the 5.5 m figure (0.00265, 0.00255) is close to the true centre value
by coincidence: the 1.0 m footing story drifts about as much as 2F. It is not 18 % low
here. What the centre-of-mass method does miss:
  - the GF story itself, which has no diaphragm and so is never reported. Its ETABS
    drift (0.00276 X, 0.00294 Y) is as large as 2F's, and it governs the wind case;
  - torsion: the corner and ETABS values in Y (0.00301) are 18 % above the centre value.

B. Wind
-------
WX and WY are the same pattern twice (B1): both 'Create All' at angle 0. Each expands
into the same 12 sub-patterns covering both directions. Base reactions, both cases:
step 1 FX -142.2 kN (ASCE case 1, angle 0); step 2 FY -295.7 kN (case 1, angle 90).
Plan 18.0 m (X) by 10.0 m (Y), not square. So the identical drift is expected from how
the patterns are defined, and the wind combinations are run twice for nothing.

C. Footing-level columns
------------------------
From the workbook (C1): lu = 500 mm; phi Vn 835 kN (600x600), 1,114 kN (600x800).
  GF-C1  (corner, 600x600): Ve 8,869 to 9,224 kN, ratio 10.6 to 11.0
  GF-C2  (edge, 600x600):   Ve 2,472 to 2,596 kN, ratio 3.0 to 3.1
  GF-C2A (600x800):         Ve 3,676 to 3,942 kN, ratio 3.2 to 3.4
My reading, not checked in the code: Ve x lu for GF-C1 is 9,224 x 0.5 = 4,612 kN-m, far
more than two Mpr of a 600x600 column with 16D25 can be. For GF-C2, 2,600 x 0.5 =
1,300 kN-m is of the order of one Mpr. The beams at the top joint are tie beams with
2D28 (sum of Mn 203 to 406 kN-m in the 2-C2 bottom rows of D1), so a cap by the beam
moments at the top would give far less than either. I could not tell from the workbook
which moments were used; it has no Mpr columns. The corner column having the largest Ve
is the odd part.
With 'check the foundation level: No' (C2) the 12 failures go away: 25 pass, 22 fail.

D. Joint shear
--------------
From D1. Both joints are 600x600 columns with f'c 27.59 MPa and G_500X500 girders
(500 wide, 500 deep) carrying 4D28 top and 4D28 bottom.
Worked back from the workbook (my arithmetic):
  - Capacity: 1,928.76 kN = 0.85 x 1.2 x sqrt(27.59) x 600 x 600 / 1000. So phi = 0.85,
    the coefficient is 1.2 (MPa units; ACI 18.8.4.1 'three faces or two opposite
    faces') and Aj is the full 600 x 600 column. At the top of 3-C1A the capacity is
    1,339.42 kN = 0.85 x 1.2 x sqrt(27.59) x 500 x 500: there the 500x500 column above
    (RD-C1A) is used. Both directions get the same coefficient, with one beam or two.
  - Demand: one beam, 1.25 x 413.69 x 2,463 mm2 (4D28) = 1,273.7 kN less the column
    shear gives 1,109 kN (column shear 164 kN); two beams give exactly twice, 2,218 kN.
    So with two beams both the top bars of one and the bottom bars of the other are
    taken, as you said.
  - Is 4D28 needed? Top Mu at these ends: 194 to 198 kN-m at 2F, 160 to 162 at 3F
    (GX girders), 128 to 170 (GY). Bottom Mu 61 to 124 kN-m. Rough hand check with
    d = 440 mm: phi Mn is about 192 kN-m for 2D28, 280 for 3D28, 360 for 4D28. So
    strength needs 3 bars on top at 2F and 2 elsewhere, and 2 at the bottom. The 4 top
    and 4 bottom come from something other than strength (a minimum number of bars for
    the 500 mm width, or a detailing rule); the workbook does not say which. The two
    SMRF checks are PASS. Halving the bars would roughly halve the joint demand.

E. Default deflection combinations
----------------------------------
See E1. The default of each of the four role dialogs is a factored ULS combination,
and 'Let the toolkit add it' is the last option. A user who presses Enter four times
gets deflections from 1.4 DL and 1.2 DL + 1.6 LL. The run still reports 0 failing.

F. Earth cover
--------------

the simulation needs a model with a 250 mm beam; none here.
